# OSINT Tools

A quick-reference list of OSINT (Open Source Intelligence) search queries and tools.

## 1. Google Dorking - Find a Name in Files

Search for documents that mention a target's name, restricted to common file types:

```text
"Name" filetype:pdf OR filetype:doc OR filetype:docx OR filetype:txt OR filetype:rtf OR filetype:ppt OR filetype:pptx OR filetype:xls OR filetype:xlsx OR filetype:csv
```

## 2. Social Media & Username Lookup

- Google dork: `-site:instagram.com intext:nyalas_`
- [instantusername.com](https://instantusername.com/) - Check username availability across platforms
- [fingerprint.to](https://fingerprint.to/)
- [inteltechniques.com/tools/Instagram.html](https://inteltechniques.com/tools/Instagram.html) - Instagram OSINT tool
- [epieos.com](https://epieos.com/) - Lookup by phone numbers and emails
- [whatsmyname.io](https://whatsmyname.io/) - Username search across sites
